Research Overview
======

My research focuses on cyber resilience for Cyber-Physical Artificial Intelligence (CPAI)—the development of AI-enabled cyber-physical systems (CPS) that are secure, resilient, and trustworthy. I study cyber resilience of CPAI including Energy and Utilities, Transportation and Logistics (maritime, aviation and land transport), Emergency Services, manufacturing and robotics. <!-- CPAI systems are driven by advanced AI models embedded within integrated systems. These systems combine cyber-elements—including AI, and secure communication networks—to orchestrate intelligent sensors and adaptive actuators, enabling autonomous decision-making and safe physical interaction.-->  The integration of AI, software, communication, and computing in the CPAI increases vulnerability to cyber attacks, especially as many systems rely on legacy components not designed for security or resilience. 

***Keywords: Cyber-Physical AI Resilience, Cyber-Physical Systems Security, Embodied AI Security, Industrial Control Systems Security, Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) and Operational Technology (OT) security***
